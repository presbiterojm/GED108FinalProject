What I Took Away

Lessons from GED108 Art Appreciation

01 Look twice

Icarus taught me the main subject can be the thing you almost miss. Art rewards slowing down.

02 Culture changes the picture

A Water Margin warrior and a Washington portrait both show heroes, just in totally different ways.

03 Art answers its time

Op Art came out of a restless 1960s and space-age tech. Nothing is made in a vacuum.

04 Making it myself

Recreating Rodin's Thinker made me notice every choice an artist makes.
