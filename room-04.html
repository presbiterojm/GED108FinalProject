Room 04: Integration of Global Culture

THE CULTURE

Urban America in the 1940s, right after the U.S. entered WWII. Four people in a bright diner on a dark corner show individualism and city isolation. The diner is a "third place" for night owls and shift workers.

HOW IT CHANGED ME

The diner has no visible exit. Sitting next to someone does not mean you know what they feel, and quiet solitude is something everyone goes through.

THE ROLE OF ART

Art is a shared language. Light, shadow and framing show a mood most people understand, so different cultures can see that our emotional needs are the same.
