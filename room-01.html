Room 01: Visual Analysis

WHAT I SAW

A calm countryside with a plowman, a shepherd and a ship. Icarus is just a pair of tiny legs in the corner.

WHAT I FOUND

Bruegel uses scale and placement to pull your eye away from Icarus, so you have to hunt for him.

WHAT IT MEANS

Tragedy can happen right next to us while life keeps going. Some people struggle in silence.
